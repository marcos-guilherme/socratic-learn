# Learner Profile

## Current context
A fictional learner is beginning the mathematical preparation needed to reason
about attention mechanisms.

## Existing experience
The learner can add and scale two-dimensional vectors and can calculate vector
lengths, but has not yet demonstrated a reliable interpretation of dot products.

## Preferred interaction
The learner prefers one focused question at a time, followed by a chance to
revise an answer before receiving an explanation.

## Accessibility and constraints
Use plain-text notation that remains readable without diagram rendering.

## Topics to avoid or defer
Defer implementation details of full attention layers until the dot-product
prerequisite is demonstrated.
