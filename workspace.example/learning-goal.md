# Learning Goal

## Interest
Understand why attention mechanisms use dot products to compare vectors.

## Observable outcome
Given two small vectors, compute their dot product and explain what a larger or
smaller result says about their alignment without treating it as an angle.

## Motivation
Use the result later to reason about query-key compatibility scores.

## Available time
One 30-minute session for the prerequisite, followed by a short transfer check.

## Desired depth
Build a geometric and algebraic interpretation; defer matrix-scale implementation.

## Completion evidence
Correctly solve a new dot-product case and justify the interpretation from signs,
magnitudes, and the formula.
